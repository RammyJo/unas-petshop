Welcome to Our Shop

Una's Petshop is a small neighborhood pet shop in San Fernando, La Union. We're known for ornamental fish — ranchu, koi, channa, bettas and livebearers — and we've also stocked birds like parakeets and African lovebirds, plus aquariums and supplies.

We're open every day, 9AM to 6PM. The friendliest way to see our newest arrivals is our Facebook page — or simply walk in and say hi. Feel free to drop by, mga suki!
